How to Choose a Real Estate Team (Anywhere in the Country)

By Steven Smith · November 18, 2026 · 1 min read

Whether you're buying in Baton Rouge or selling in Boise, choosing an agent or team works the same way. Most people pick the first name they recognize. A few good questions will do better.

1. Ask for verified numbers

Anyone can say "top producer." Ask for third-party verified production, such as RealTrends Verified, not estimates. For reference, Team Smith's RealTrends Verified 2025 numbers are 322 sides and $109.14 million in volume. Whoever you interview should be able to show you theirs.

2. Read the reviews, then read more

Look for consistent themes across hundreds of reviews on more than one site, not a few glowing ones. We have 600+ five-star reviews on Zillow and 150+ on Google, and we encourage you to read the ones that mention situations like yours.

3. Find out who actually does the work

Teams can be a real advantage: more eyes, more systems, faster response. But ask who your day-to-day contact is, how fast they respond, and what happens when they're on vacation.

4. Ask about the plan, not the pitch

  • How will you price my home, and what data backs it up?
  • What's your marketing plan in the first 14 days?
  • How do you handle multiple offers or a low appraisal?

5. Trust the conversation

You should come away understanding your options, including the ones that don't earn the agent a commission. If you don't, keep interviewing.
