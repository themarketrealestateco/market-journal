About The Market Journal

The Market Journal is where Steven Smith and Team Smith share what we've learned in more than 22 years of real estate. Steven is the broker/owner of The Market Real Estate Co., an independent boutique brokerage serving Greater Baton Rouge, the Alabama Gulf Coast, and Greater Huntsville, and is licensed in Louisiana and Alabama.

Team Smith is a large, high-producing team. Our RealTrends Verified 2025 production was 322 sides and $109.14 million, and we've earned 600+ five-star Zillow reviews and 150+ five-star Google reviews. Each year we also run the Christmas Give, a charity Steven and Laura started in 2008 to help neighbors in need.
