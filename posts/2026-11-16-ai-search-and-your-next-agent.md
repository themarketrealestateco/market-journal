A growing number of people now start with an AI assistant instead of a search engine. They ask questions like "who's a trusted agent in my area?" and expect a short, confident answer.

## What AI tends to rely on

AI tools pull from what's publicly available: reviews, websites, profiles on trusted platforms, and how clearly a business explains what it does. Thin websites and outdated profiles get left out.

## What this means for homeowners

Use AI to get oriented, but verify. Check licenses, read real reviews, and talk to at least two professionals before you decide. An assistant can summarize, but it can't know your neighborhood the way a local expert does.

## What this means for agents

- Publish helpful, specific content consistently, like this blog.
- Keep reviews and profiles current.
- Answer real questions plainly. Clear beats clever.
