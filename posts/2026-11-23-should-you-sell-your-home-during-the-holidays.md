title: Should You Sell Your Home During the Holidays or Wait Until Spring?

If you have a good reason to sell now, the holidays are a workable time to list. Fewer homes are on the market, and the buyers who are out looking tend to be serious. If your timeline is flexible, spring may bring more traffic, but the difference is smaller than most sellers expect.

Here is what the sources say, and how to decide for your own home.

## Do homes sell in winter?

Yes. Zillow notes that winter has traditionally been the slowest season for home sales, especially in colder climates, but says homes are still likely to attract buyers during the holidays. Freddie Mac makes a similar point: there are typically fewer buyers in a winter market, which can mean less competition for your listing, and the buyers who house hunt in winter are typically more serious.

Redfin looked at this directly. In an analysis of more than 7 million homes listed from 2010 through 2014, 39% of homes listed in spring went under contract within 30 days, and winter was just one percentage point behind. Redfin's conclusion was that there is not a huge advantage or disadvantage to listing in any season, since the numbers vary by just a few percentage points. That data is more than a decade old, so treat it as a pattern, not a forecast for your street. Your local market will tell you more.

## What are the advantages of listing now?

- **Less competition.** With fewer homes for sale, yours is easier to find. Freddie Mac says sellers will not have to vie for buyers' attention as much.
- **Motivated buyers.** Zillow says winter buyers are likely to be motivated, and Freddie Mac notes that year-end bonuses and overtime can add to some buyers' purchasing power.
- **Buyers are not tied to the school calendar.** Zillow, citing its 2024 Consumer Housing Trends Report, says only 36% of buyers have kids under 18 at home.
- **Weather shows what your home can do.** A warm, well-lit home on a cold evening is a strong showing.

## What are the downsides?

- **Fewer showings.** Short days, cold weather, and travel plans mean fewer people out looking, especially in the last two weeks of December.
- **Flaws show up.** Zillow points out that drafty, dark, or leaky spots show themselves more in the cold months.
- **Timelines can slip.** Zillow says financing can be delayed by vacation schedules and bank holidays, so build in extra time.
- **Fewer buyers means fewer offers.** Zillow notes you are less likely to get multiple offers, so price carefully from day one.

## How do I decide whether to list now or wait?

Start with your reason for selling, not the calendar. A few questions help:

- Do you have a deadline, such as a job change or a purchase you need to make? If so, waiting for spring may cost more than it gains.
- Is your home ready? If repairs, cleaning, or photos will take another month, listing in early January may beat rushing it now.
- Are similar homes near you selling? Ask your agent for recent sales and how long homes took to go under contract. Local data matters more than national seasons.
- Can you carry the house longer if it takes more time? If not, a firm plan for pricing matters more than the month.

In our experience, a well-priced, well-prepared home draws attention in any month, and the sellers who struggle are usually the ones who priced for the season they hoped for instead of the market they have.

## How should I prepare a home for a winter listing?

- **Light it up.** Daylight is limited, so turn on lamps and open blinds before every showing. Freddie Mac suggests winter greenery and warm lights to show off the best parts of your home.
- **Keep it warm and safe.** Clear snow, ice, and leaves from walkways, and set the heat so the home feels comfortable. Zillow suggests clearing leftover leaves once fall ends.
- **Use good exterior photos.** Zillow recommends using a picture from the previous spring or summer for the exterior if the yard looks bare now.
- **Fix drafts and leaks first.** Winter inspections and showings will find them anyway.
- **Be flexible on showings.** Buyers out in the cold are usually serious, so make it easy to see the home.

## The takeaway

Sell now if you have a reason to, and your home is ready. Wait if your home is not ready or your timeline is open. Either way, pricing and preparation matter more than the month. Talk with a local agent about recent sales near you before you decide.
