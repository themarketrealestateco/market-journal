After more than 22 years in this business, the same questions come up in nearly every listing conversation.

## What's my home worth?

It's what a buyer will pay in today's market, based on recent comparable sales, condition, and location, not what you owe or what a neighbor hoped for.

## How long will it take?

It depends on price, condition, and local demand. A well-priced, well-presented home typically gets its strongest interest early, which is why the first couple of weeks matter so much.

## What should I fix before listing?

Usually less than you think. Focus on cleanliness, clutter, curb appeal, and anything an inspector will flag. Big renovations rarely return what they cost right before a sale.

## What will it cost me?

Ask for a net sheet up front. It shows what you'd walk away with after commissions, closing costs, and payoff, so there are no surprises.

## Should I buy first or sell first?

There are good paths for either. Talk with your agent and your lender early so the timing works for you.
